数独求解器Sudoku Solver Sudoku Solver数独求解器

拍一张报纸、书上的数独，或者截一张 App 的图，约 1 秒出答案；没有图片也能手动输入。OpenCV 与角点热力图 CNN 找网格，3.8 万参数的 CNN 读数字，用唯一解来检验识别结果。 Snap a puzzle from a newspaper, a book or an app and get the solution in about a second, or type it in. OpenCV and a corner-heatmap CNN find the grid, a 38K-parameter CNN reads the digits, and the unique solution checks the reading.

三国墨英传水墨风三国策略游戏An ink-painting Three Kingdoms strategy game

玩法复刻《三国群英传3》，内政、外交、野战、百人战、单挑都已实现。画面、音乐和数据全部由代码生成，不含原版内容；有原版安装包的话，也可以在浏览器本地导入原版素材。电脑、手机、平板都能玩。 A browser remake of the gameplay of Romance of the Three Kingdoms: Heroes 3 (2002), with domestic affairs, diplomacy, field battles, hundred-soldier battles and duels. Art, music and data are generated by code; owners of the original can import its assets locally. Plays on desktop, phone and tablet.
